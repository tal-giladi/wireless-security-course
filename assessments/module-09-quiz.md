# Module 9 quiz — Rogue AP, evil twin & client-side

This is the quiz for Module 9. It checks that you can reason about rogue-AP attacks the way the module insists on: evil-twin success is **conditional on the client's behaviour and configuration**, a captive portal that harvests a passphrase is **social engineering, not a crypto break**, and KARMA/MANA/known-beacon are **PNL-dependent** techniques that modern clients largely resist.

- **Format:** multiple choice, one correct answer each.
- **Pass mark:** 70%.
- **Covers:** [09.1 rogue-AP mechanics & lures](../lessons/module-09/lesson-01.md),
  [09.2 evil twin — conditional on client behaviour](../lessons/module-09/lesson-02.md),
  [09.3 captive portals — social engineering](../lessons/module-09/lesson-03.md),
  [09.4 KARMA, MANA & known-beacon](../lessons/module-09/lesson-04.md),
  [09.5 detecting & defending against rogue APs](../lessons/module-09/lesson-05.md).

The questions are scenario-based and reward precise, qualified claims — a conditional "a client that does X is vulnerable to Y" over a universal one, and an honest account of what was actually demonstrated. Remember that you can pass the defence material by showing a protection works (a hardened client that does not auto-join the twin).
