# Module 15 quiz — Adjacent wireless (elective, awareness)

This is the quiz for the **elective, awareness-only** module on wireless technologies next to Wi-Fi. It
checks that you can place BLE/Bluetooth, Zigbee/Thread, RFID/NFC, and SDR at a high level, describe
their security concerns honestly, and — importantly — respect the scope and legal limits (especially
that this module is awareness, not a deep attack course, and that SDR transmitting is heavily
regulated).

- **Format:** multiple choice, one correct answer each.
- **Pass mark:** 70%.
- **Covers:** [15.1 BLE/Bluetooth](../lessons/module-15/lesson-01.md),
  [15.2 Zigbee, Thread, RFID, NFC](../lessons/module-15/lesson-02.md),
  [15.3 SDR](../lessons/module-15/lesson-03.md).

The questions reward accurate scoping — "awareness, owned devices, check the law, go deeper in a
dedicated resource" — over any claim to hands-on mastery of these adjacent technologies.
