# Module 4 quiz — Wireless reconnaissance

This quiz checks that you can reason about reconnaissance the way an engagement demands: choosing the quietest discovery method that answers the question, reading what airodump-ng and Kismet actually show you, understanding what clients reveal and how modern privacy features change that, and staying on the right side of the law when discovery scales to mapping.

- **Format:** multiple choice, one correct answer each.
- **Pass mark:** 70%.
- **Covers:** [04.1 passive vs active discovery](../lessons/module-04/lesson-01.md),
  [04.2 airodump-ng and Kismet](../lessons/module-04/lesson-02.md),
  [04.3 clients, probe requests, and the PNL](../lessons/module-04/lesson-03.md),
  [04.4 wardriving, WiGLE, and recon ethics](../lessons/module-04/lesson-04.md).

The questions are scenario-based: they ask you to apply recon reasoning — what transmits, what a display means, what a client exposes, and what you may lawfully collect — to realistic situations, not to recall definitions. If the channel-hop-versus-lock or the conditional evil-twin reasoning gives you trouble, re-read 04.2 and 04.3 before moving on; Modules 5 and 6 assume you can pick a target off a survey and capture from it cleanly.
