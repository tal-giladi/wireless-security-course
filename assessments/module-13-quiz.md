# Module 13 quiz — Wireless engagements & methodology

> **Core / professional-practice module.** This quiz checks the judgement a wireless engagement is built
> on: scoping honestly around a medium that ignores property lines, running one repeatable methodology with
> defensible evidence, and turning findings into asset-tied impact with limitations stated plainly.

This module is **path R throughout** — scoping, methodology, and reporting are paper and analysis work, not
exploitation. The questions reward the honest professional position: an allowlist keyed on BSSIDs with the
collateral scoped rather than wished away; evidence with provenance, checksums, and the correct lab-path
credit; and impact that follows the client's topology to the asset, with limitations — including a technique
a control legitimately blocked — written down so that a missing finding is never read as a guarantee of safety.

- **Format:** multiple choice, one correct answer each.
- **Pass mark:** 70%.
- **Covers:** [13.1 Scoping and authorisation](../lessons/module-13/lesson-01.md),
  [13.2 Methodology and evidence capture](../lessons/module-13/lesson-02.md),
  [13.3 Impact assessment and limitations](../lessons/module-13/lesson-03.md).

A high-value answer here is the scoped, evidenced, honestly-bounded one — "here is what I proved, on which
path, and here is what I did not test and why" — as much as any recovered key.
