# Module 5 quiz — Capturing & analysing 802.11

This quiz checks that you can read a wireless capture honestly: tell the capture's layers apart, drive
Wireshark's dissection and display filters, trace a join to its handshake, judge whether that handshake
is crackable, and scope what passive capture can and cannot recover.

- **Format:** multiple choice, one correct answer each.
- **Pass mark:** 70%.
- **Covers:** [05.1 radiotap & capture formats](../lessons/module-05/lesson-01.md),
  [05.2 Wireshark for 802.11](../lessons/module-05/lesson-02.md),
  [05.3 following a join & the handshake](../lessons/module-05/lesson-03.md),
  [05.4 protected vs cleartext frames](../lessons/module-05/lesson-04.md).

The questions are scenario-based: they ask you to apply the analysis reasoning to real capture
situations — a mystery file, an empty filter, a partial handshake, an over-stated claim — rather than
recall definitions. If the handshake-completeness or readability questions trip you up, re-read 05.3
and 05.4 before Module 6, which assumes you can tell a crackable capture from a useless one.
