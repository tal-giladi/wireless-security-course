# Module 1 quiz — RF & 802.11 fundamentals

This quiz checks that you can reason about the physical and framing fundamentals the rest of the course
builds on: how radio power and antennas bound your capture range, how bands and channels constrain what
you can hear, how to read an 802.11 frame, and how a client joins a network.

- **Format:** multiple choice, one correct answer each.
- **Pass mark:** 70%.
- **Covers:** [01.1 radio basics](../lessons/module-01/lesson-01.md),
  [01.2 bands & channels](../lessons/module-01/lesson-02.md),
  [01.3 the 802.11 frame](../lessons/module-01/lesson-03.md),
  [01.4 joining a network](../lessons/module-01/lesson-04.md).

The questions are scenario-based: they ask you to apply the RF and framing reasoning to capture and
containment situations, not to recall definitions. If you struggle with the frame-reading or join
questions, re-read 01.3 and 01.4 before moving on — Modules 4 through 6 assume you can trace a join and
read addressing fluently.
