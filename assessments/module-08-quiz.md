# Module 8 quiz — WPS (elective)

This is the quiz for the **elective** WPS module. It checks that you can reason about how WPS works, why
Pixie Dust succeeds or fails on a given AP, why the online PIN brute is mostly-mitigated legacy, and why
the clean remediation is usually to disable WPS.

- **Format:** multiple choice, one correct answer each.
- **Pass mark:** 70%.
- **Covers:** [08.1 how WPS works](../lessons/module-08/lesson-01.md),
  [08.2 Pixie Dust](../lessons/module-08/lesson-02.md),
  [08.3 online PIN brute](../lessons/module-08/lesson-03.md),
  [08.4 prevalence & remediation](../lessons/module-08/lesson-04.md).

The questions are scenario-based and reward precise, qualified claims — Pixie Dust depends on the AP's
implementation, and a clean "not vulnerable / disable WPS" result is a valid, valuable outcome.
