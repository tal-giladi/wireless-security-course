# Module 0 quiz — How this course works & safe lab practice

This quiz checks that you can reason about the three lab paths, your own readiness, and — most
importantly — the safety, containment, and legal framing that every later module depends on.

- **Format:** multiple choice, one correct answer each.
- **Pass mark:** 70%.
- **Covers:** [00.1 paths & expectations](../lessons/module-00/lesson-01.md),
  [00.2 readiness](../lessons/module-00/lesson-02.md),
  [00.3 safety, containment & the law](../lessons/module-00/lesson-03.md).

The questions are scenario-based: they ask you to apply the containment and honesty rules, not to
recall definitions. If you miss the safety questions, re-read 00.3 before continuing — the rest of the
course assumes those habits.
