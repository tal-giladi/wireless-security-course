# Module 14 quiz — Defence program & reporting

This quiz checks that you can assemble the course's defence controls into an ongoing program, write a wireless penetration-test report that serves both a decision-maker and an engineer, and turn findings into a prioritised, verifiable remediation roadmap with honest residual risk.

- **Format:** multiple choice, one correct answer each.
- **Pass mark:** 70%.
- **Covers:** [the defence program](../lessons/module-14/lesson-01.md),
  [writing the wireless pentest report](../lessons/module-14/lesson-02.md),
  [remediation roadmap, residual risk & client communication](../lessons/module-14/lesson-03.md).

The questions are scenario-based: they ask you to decide how to structure a program, what belongs in a finding, how to prioritise and prove a fix, how to state residual risk, and why a recovered key is evidence rather than a deliverable — not to recall definitions. If you miss several, re-read the relevant lesson before moving on to the capstones.
