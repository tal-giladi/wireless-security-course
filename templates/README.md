# Templates

Reusable documents the lessons and capstones link to. Copy one into your own notes and fill it in;
nothing here is graded by itself. Downloading the module zip is not required to use these — they are
plain markdown.

- [Wireless engagement report](report-template.md) — the structure Module 14 teaches and both
  capstones require: executive summary, scope, methodology, findings, impact, limitations, remediation,
  residual risk.
- [Scope & authorisation](authorisation-template.md) — what must be agreed and written down **before**
  any wireless testing: targets you own/are authorised for, allowlist of BSSIDs/SSIDs, time window,
  transmission limits, stop conditions.
- [Lab notebook](lab-notebook-template.md) — per-lab record of the path used (R/V/P), starting state,
  commands, artifacts, and cleanup, so your work is reproducible and honestly scoped.
